Wiring Notes

470 ohm resistor on the data line protects the first LED from voltage spikes. Place it as close to the strip's DIN pin as possible.

1000uF capacitor across the strip's power rails prevents inrush current from damaging LEDs on power-on. Observe polarity (+ to 5V).

INMP441 L/R pin is tied to GND. The firmware reads I2S_CHANNEL_FMT_ONLY_RIGHT, which is the setting that actually produces samples on this wiring. ONLY_LEFT was tried first and returned silence.

Buttons use ESP32 internal pull-ups (INPUT_PULLUP). Press connects to GND (active LOW). No external resistors needed.

For breadboard testing: power everything from USB-C on the ESP32. The onboard 5V pin provides USB voltage directly to the strip.

For wearable build: add 18650 + TP4056 (charging) + MT3608 (boost to 5V) + slide switch. See bottom section of diagram.
